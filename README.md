Computer Engineer working as a **Cybersecurity Consultant**, where my day-to-day is **GRC and security governance** — risk analysis, hardening and regulatory compliance (ENS, NIS2, ISO 27001/22301) for public- and private-sector clients.

Alongside the consultancy work, I keep a strong technical line in **offensive security and hardware hacking**: pulling firmware off devices, reverse engineering embedded binaries, and hunting vulnerabilities in IoT. It's the part of the field I research for its own sake.

Currently pursuing the **Inter-University Master's in Cybersecurity (MUNICS, UDC / UVigo)**.

### What I work on

- **Firmware & embedded RE** — SPI flash dumping (CH341A), image extraction and carving, static/dynamic reversing of MIPS / ARM / Xtensa targets.
- **IoT security research** — auditing embedded devices for auth bypasses, crypto misuse, exposed debug/diagnostic surfaces and OTA weaknesses.
- **GRC & security governance** — risk analysis (MAGERIT), system hardening, secure architecture and Zero Trust, compliance with ENS · NIS2 · ISO 27001/22301.

### Selected work

- 🏆 **Best Bachelor's Thesis** — VII *Premios Cátedra R en Ciberseguridade* (UDC–UVigo), Nov 2025, for *"Security analysis and vulnerability research in IoT devices"* (graded 9.8/10): a practical, low-cost methodology for assessing home-IoT firmware security.
